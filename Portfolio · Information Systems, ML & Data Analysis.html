Project 01 · Google Colab Notebook

Restaurant Data Analysis

Analysis of a Zomato restaurant dataset (9,551 records, 21 columns) covering locations, cuisines, prices, customer ratings and online delivery, to surface market trends and customer preferences.

PythonPandasMatplotlibGoogle Colab
  • New Delhi has the most restaurants (5,473), followed by Gurgaon and Noida
  • Higher price ranges tend to have higher average ratings (2.00 for range 1, 3.82 for range 4)
  • Restaurants with online delivery average 3.25 vs 2.47 without
  • North Indian is the most common cuisine, then Chinese and Fast Food
